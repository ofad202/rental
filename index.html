House Rules & Reminders

  • Valid ID & Contract Signing: Tenants must present a valid government ID and sign a 1-year lease contract before moving in.
  • Annual Contract Renewal: The lease contract is for 1 year and must be renewed annually.
  • No Smoking: Smoking is strictly prohibited inside the units and within the compound.
  • No Illegal Drugs / Activities: Illegal drugs, gambling, and any unlawful activities are strictly prohibited.
  • No Drunkards / Loitering: Avoid loud noises and drinking outside the units. Respect the peace of your neighbors.
  • Street Parking Only: No private parking space. You may park on the street, but "Park at your own risk".
  • 1 Month Advance & 1 Month Deposit: These must be settled before moving in.
  • Utility Bills: Each unit has its own direct water and electric connection. Please pay your bills on time.
