House Rules & Reminders

  • Strictly No Smoking: Smoking is prohibited inside the units and within the compound.
  • No Illegal Activities: Illegal drugs, gambling, and any unlawful activities are strictly prohibited.
  • No Pets Allowed: Pets are not permitted inside the units or within the compound premises.
  • Quiet Hours & Peace: Please observe silence from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM. No loud music, videoke, or loitering/drinking outside the units. Respect your neighbors.
  • No Subleasing / Bed-spacing: Tenants are not allowed to sublease the unit or accept boarders without the landlord's written consent. Unauthorized subleasing is grounds for contract termination.
  • Visitor Policy: You must inform the landlord beforehand if you have overnight visitors. Sudden additions to occupants are prohibited.
  • Street Parking Only: No private parking space. You may park on the street, but "Park at your own risk".
